# Before you send it to Buttercup ♡

Here are the parts that will make the biggest difference:

- **Photos:** use photos that Que would recognize immediately — your first photo, a goofy one, a day together, something tiny you both remember.
- **Story:** edit the example chapters with your actual important moments and dates. Avoid leaving placeholders in the final gift.
- **Letter:** say precisely what you're sorry for and how you'll work to change it. Don't promise something you cannot follow through on. Give her room to feel what she feels.
- **Signature:** replace "Your person ♡" with your usual affectionate sign-off.
- **Song:** use a song you both like, or keep the included original instrumental.
- **Publish:** in Edit mode choose **Export gift-data.json**, copy it into `public/`, commit and push the file to GitHub, and test in an incognito window.

There is no long-distance section. This is about Que, here with you, and your hope to reconnect with more care.
